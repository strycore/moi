Framework Laptop 16 — Quick Start Guide

1. Insert Expansion Cards into the bays on either side until they click.
2. Install Input Modules (keyboard, numpad, RGB LED Matrix spacer) onto the
   midplate magnets; they seat flush and lock automatically.
3. To fit the Graphics Module, power off, remove the rear Expansion Bay shell,
   slide in the module, and re-seat the shell.

Powering on: hold the power button (top-right of the keyboard deck) for 1s.
Charging: USB-C PD, 180W adapter included. Any USB-C port may be used to charge.
Recovery: hold F2 during boot for BIOS; the bootloader menu is on F12.
